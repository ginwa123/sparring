Method & limitations

  • Sandbox: --cpus=2 --memory=512m --memory-swap=512m --pids-limit=512 --ulimit nofile=4096:4096, loopback-only publish. wrk (-t4, 10 s, 3 reps, alternating framework order, 2 s cooldown) runs on the host. Endpoints identical on all servers (/health, /hello/:name, /users, /api/v1/echo, /api/v1/product/:id (~1KB doc), /api/v1/checkout (~0.6KB cart)); mix = 70/20/10 Lua script (scripts/wrk-mix.lua), realistic = 50/50 product/checkout (scripts/wrk-realistic.lua).
  • Runtime sizing pinned to the quota: GOMAXPROCS=2 (fiber), Tokio worker threads = 2 (axum); .NET is cgroup-aware (no pinning needed).
  • Single host, loopback only, no TLS, minimal bench servers (no SSE/WS/templates).
  • Raw logs: results/wrk_*.txt + results/wrk_runs.log.
